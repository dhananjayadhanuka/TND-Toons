WELCOME TO YOUR STUDIO

A little story, step by step.

  1. Choose your cast.

    Click a character card to add them. Click a character on the stage to edit their own track.

  2. Choose a moment.

    Click the timeline ruler. Set an expression, move the character, or change their size. A keyframe is saved at that time.

  3. Bring them to life.

    Add a motion clip. Position and size blend between keyframes; expressions switch at their keyframes.

  4. Tell your story.

    Choose a background, add narrator audio, preview and export. Save a project file to keep a portable copy.
